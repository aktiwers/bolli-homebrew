A YouTube app for LG webOS TVs. It looks and works like YouTube's own TV app, but it's fast on old TVs, has ad block and skips sponsor segments.

  • Home, Subscriptions, Library, Gaming, Podcasts, Music, Movies and Sports, like YouTube's TV app
  • Its own player: up to 4K, chapters, subtitles (also auto-translated), dubbed audio tracks and stable volume
  • Skips sponsors, intros and the like with SponsorBlock
  • Ad block
  • Previews when you rest on a video
  • Search with YouTube's suggestions
  • Cast from YouTube on your phone
  • More than one account, with "Who's watching?"
  • No Shorts

Bolli YouTube is not made by or connected to YouTube or Google. You sign in with your own Google account.
