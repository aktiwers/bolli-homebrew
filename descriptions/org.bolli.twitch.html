A Twitch app for LG webOS TVs. It looks and works like Twitch's own TV app, but it's fast on old TVs and has no ads.

  • Home, Following, Browse and Search, like Twitch's TV app
  • No ads: live streams and past broadcasts in full quality, up to 1080p60
  • Chat next to the stream, with emotes and badges
  • Past broadcasts and clips on every channel page
  • Sign in with a code from your phone
  • Search with suggestions as you type

Bolli Twitch is not made by or connected to Twitch. You can watch without an account, or sign in with your own Twitch account.
